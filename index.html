HOW TO PLAY

  • 🕹️ Left thumb: drag anywhere on the left to run and dribble.
  • 🔴 Defenders flash a red lane before they slide-tackle. Get out of that lane!
  • ✨ TRICK near a defender = nutmeg through their legs. Away from one = spin dash.
  • 🤝 ONE-TWO: pass to Mia on the wing and she returns it ahead of you. Can't be tackled while the ball is away.
  • 🔥 Every trick heats the ball up. Full heat = FLAMING COMET: it knocks defenders flat and burns the keeper's gloves.
  • 🥅 In the box a target slides across the goal. TAP when it is outside the keeper's reach.
  • ⭐ Earn stars: score, keep all your lives, and pull off enough tricks. Spend coins on ball skins.
